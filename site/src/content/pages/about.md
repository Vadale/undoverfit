I am **Alessandro Vadalà**. I work on data analysis with R and build tools with the help of AI. My background is in economics; I am currently studying for a master's degree in Public and Health Economics at the University of Molise.

**Undoverfit** is where I write about data, statistics, economics, technology and the decisions behind my projects. The name comes from a familiar problem: a model can miss what matters or fit the noise so closely that it stops being useful. I like that question beyond statistics, too: how much should we trust an explanation, and what would make us change our minds?

## What you will find here

Practical examples of working with data, explanations of statistical ideas, and notes on using AI to build and improve software. I want to make R more approachable, including for people who do not think of themselves as programmers. Expect the question, the choices and the limitations alongside the code.

I write in English to make these conversations accessible beyond Italy. You are welcome to get in touch in Italian or English.

## What I am working on

- **[relm](https://github.com/Vadale/R-ebirth)**: an open-source R package for working with local language models, including extracting structured information from documents.
- **[R Statistical Analysis Skill](https://github.com/Vadale/r-statistical-analysis-skill)** and other skills for AI agents: reusable instructions and tools for analysis, project planning and reviewing what belongs in public material.
- **Research on municipal communications and financial indicators**, intended to form the basis of my master's thesis.

Earlier, I developed **NoRoom**, a privacy project spanning a browser extension, a web application, backend services and tracker data. The initiative is currently on hold. You can find more context on the [projects page](/projects).

## How I work

I use AI assistants throughout development. I define the problem, choose priorities and constraints, coordinate the work, and review the results through examples, tests and documentation. I care about what a tool lets someone do, what can go wrong, and how much time and money it takes to maintain.

This blog is also a place to explain those decisions: what I kept, what I changed and what I stopped doing. Outside these projects, I am interested in cinema, theatre and board games.
